# Business Overview

Vieira's Gym is a fitness centre located in Maputo, Mozambique specialising in martial arts, functional fitness, strength training and athlete development.

The gym welcomes beginners, recreational members and competitive athletes, providing professional coaching in an environment built around discipline, consistency and continuous improvement.

The business consistently promotes physical development together with mental resilience, teamwork and personal growth.
